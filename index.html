About me

Turning complex challenges into practical, people-friendly solutions

I bring together two worlds that rarely meet: enterprise technology and hands-on retail operations. My career began in software consulting in New Delhi and grew through large-scale systems for organizations like Garmin, EDS and USDA programs, into change and release management at a U.S. federal agency, alongside co-owning a retail business.

Alongside that work, I co-own and help run retail businesses in Kansas and Missouri, including gas stations and convenience stores. I know the daily realities first-hand: closing the store, reconciling cash, reading the sales report and making sure numbers add up.

Today I'm focused on using practical AI and automation to make everyday work simpler for independent retailers, and I stay committed to lifelong learning about personal growth, self-awareness and empowerment.
